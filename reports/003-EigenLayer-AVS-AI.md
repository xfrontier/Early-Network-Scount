### 2. Value Capture Analysis: Which Entities Capture the Value?

When decentralized AI protocols require economic security and verifiable compute without building their own L1 blockchain, value flows directly to **3 key entities**:

* **Entity A: Restaking Protocol & AVS Infrastructure (EigenLayer Ecosystem)**
  * **Mechanism**: Charges a protocol fee on all AVS security payments made by AI networks purchasing restaked cryptoeconomic security.
  * **Value Accrual**: Restaking governance tokens (EIGEN/Liquid Restaking Tokens - LRTs) and protocol DAO reserves.
* **Entity B: Node Operators & Restakers (ETH / Liquid Restaking Stakers)**
  * **Mechanism**: ETH stakers delegate their assets to AI AVS operators to run inference verification, earning additional service yield and penalty/slashing protection.
  * **Value Accrual**: Enhanced native yield on stETH/eETH and native AVS token reward distributions.
* **Entity C: AI Coprocessor & Zero-Knowledge Inference Networks (e.g., Lagrange, Ritual)**
  * **Mechanism**: Monetizes verifiable AI model execution (zkML) for smart contracts that require trustless machine learning outputs.
  * **Value Accrual**: Token valuations of specialized AI coprocessors and protocol treasury revenue.
